The Hidden Crisis in Rural India

India accounts for nearly one-fifth of all global maternal deaths, with rural and underprivileged communities bearing the largest burden.

  • Lack of Access: Millions of pregnant women live far from primary health centers.
  • The "Dai" Shortage: Traditional midwives (Dais) are culturally crucial but increasingly unaffordable or unavailable for continuous care.
  • Nutritional Deficiencies: Anemia and malnutrition during pregnancy lead directly to high mortality rates.
  • Digital Divide: Most existing health apps require high-speed internet, which fails in remote villages.

Transforming Clinical Care

For Doctors, Hospitals, and Clinics:
Mamta doesn't replace doctors; it extends their reach. Hospitals can onboard high-risk patients onto Mamta. The app acts as an continuous monitoring agent, answering the mother's daily anxious queries and tracking her nutrition seamlessly.

Doctors can directly access weekly summaries of the mother's symptoms, dietary habits, and vitals via a centralized dashboard without requiring her to travel miles for daily monitorings. Early intervention saves lives.
